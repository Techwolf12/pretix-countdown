0.1.0 (2026-10-20)
------------------

First release.

- Show a live countdown on the shop's front page until the start of presale.
  In an event series, each date counts down to its own presale start.
- Show a countdown on products and variations until their "Available from"
  date.
- Reload the page when a countdown reaches zero, so visitors can buy right
  away.
- Choose between clock, compact and written-out countdown formats, or write
  your own with placeholders and optional parts.
- Choose a short, long or custom date format. It is used for ``{date}`` and
  shown instead of the countdown when JavaScript is not available, e.g. in the
  embedded widget.
- Customize the countdown heading and the product text.
- Link to the settings from the plugin list.
- Requires pretix 2026.3.0 or newer.
